Questions we get asked

Is the audit really free?

Yes. You get a recorded video of your Instagram either way. If you want our team to run it after that, we talk. If not, the fixes are yours to use.

Will I have to be on camera?

Only if your strategy calls for it. Some brands grow on the owner’s face, others on product, customers or the space itself. We decide that after studying your business.

What does it cost?

In India our social media growth system starts at ₹35,000 a month + GST, and adding Meta ads takes it to ₹75,000–1,20,000 a month plus ad spend. Outside India we price to the scope your market needs. Every price is on the services page.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We plan for your market, not ours: your local competitors, buyers, platforms and ad costs. Calls happen in your working hours.

Who will I actually work with?

Our strategist, Uday Dialani, sets the strategy for every account. Our team handles content, shoots, editing and ads, and you get one point of contact.

How soon will we see results?

We agree the numbers that matter in the first month (enquiries, bookings, sales) and review them with you every month. Studio24’s 20x came in one season, and it was built on the season before it.
